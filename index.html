Privacy Policy

Smart Shopping Calculator · Effective date: November 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Smart Shopping Calculator (the “App”), published by [BLACKZT STONE COMMERCE INC], handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account. In its current version, the App does not collect, transmit, or share personal information with the developer or with third parties. The App does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs and does not make network requests.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The App saves your recent calculation history on your device so you can review it later. This history may include the calculation type, the values or description you entered, the calculated result, and the time of the calculation. The App keeps up to 100 recent records in its private application storage. The App does not send this history to the developer or to an App server.

3. Android Backup

The App currently allows Android system backup. If you have enabled device or Google backup, Android may include the App's private data, which can include calculation history, in a system backup. Backup and restore are managed by Android, your device manufacturer, and the backup settings on your device. The developer does not operate or access those backup services.

4. How to Delete Your Information

You can delete saved calculation history at any time by opening the History section in the App and selecting the clear-history option. Uninstalling the App removes its local app data from the device, subject to any system backups managed by Android or your device provider. To manage or remove backups, use your device's backup settings.

5. Sharing and Third-Party Services

The App does not sell, rent, or share your calculation history or personal information. The current version does not integrate advertising, analytics, or other third-party data-collection services.

6. Data Security

Calculation history is stored in Android's private application storage, which is isolated from the ordinary storage of other apps by the Android operating system. The App does not transmit this history to a developer-operated server. Device security and system backup settings remain under your control and the control of your device provider.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is a general-purpose shopping calculator and is not designed to collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect personal information in its current version, it does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the App's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published at this same URL with a revised effective date.
